## Detailed Description

HumanType lets you paste content into a side panel and have it typed into any web text field — automatically, and with believable human behavior.

Instead of instant paste, HumanType simulates the rhythm of a real typist: variable keystroke timing, thinking pauses, burst typing, occasional typos, and natural self-corrections. You choose a profile to match the pace you need.

**Typing Profiles**

- **Student** (~42 WPM) — casual, learning typist with longer pauses
- **Professional** (~62 WPM) — steady, experienced office typist
- **Rusher** (~75 WPM) — fast and sloppy with frequent bursts
- **SpeedBlitz** (~150 WPM) — elite/competition-level speed

**Input Modes**

- **Markdown mode** — paste markdown text; HumanType parses headings, bold, italic, and lists, then types the content with formatting shortcuts applied
- **Rich-text mode** — paste pre-formatted content from another source; formatting structure is preserved and replayed

**Works across field types**

- Plain textareas and input fields
- Rich-text editors including Google Docs
- Framework-controlled inputs (React, Vue, Angular) for plain text

**Live Progress Highlighter**

While typing, the editor switches to a read-only preview with the current segment highlighted so you can follow along in real time.

**Pause and Resume**

Pause mid-session from the side panel or with Alt+P. Resume exactly where you left off.

**Find in Editor**

Ctrl+F (or Cmd+F) opens an inline search bar with match highlighting and Prev/Next navigation.

**How it works**

1. Open the HumanType side panel from the toolbar
2. Paste or type your content into the editor
3. Select a profile and input mode
4. Click **Type It**
5. Click into the target text field within the 3-second arm window
6. HumanType types your content automatically

Click **Stop** at any time to cancel. Use **Pause** or Alt+P to hold and resume mid-session.
